Installation — four steps

  1. Open the link on the phone (or scan the QR) and accept the download.
    If Chrome shows "Dangerous download blocked": open ⋮ → Downloads, tap the blocked file and choose "Download dangerous file". Chrome flags every APK it has not seen downloaded many times; the file itself was not found harmful. Alternatives: Firefox, or Obtainium, which installs directly from GitHub.
  2. Open FlightInfo.apk from the notification or the Downloads folder.
  3. Android asks to allow installing apps from this browser — allow. One-time.
  4. If Play Protect blocks: tap "More details" → "Install anyway". This warning appears for every app installed outside the Play Store; it is not a sign of a problem.

Updates: in the app — Settings → App update → Check → Download and install. Or download again from the link above; the installation replaces the previous one and keeps your settings.

First use

  • Enter origin and destination (IATA code such as TLV, or a city name in English), or tap Scan boarding pass and point the camera at the barcode.
  • Press the REC button to record a flight log — with or without a flight plan. It pulses red while recording.
  • On board: hold the phone against the window. First GPS fix takes 30–60 s. Middle seats may get no GPS; the app then says "Predicted only" and works from time.
  • To follow a flight you are not on: enable Estimate only and enter the departure time.
  • Aerial imagery (NASA Blue Marble): Settings → Aerial imagery → Download (once, ~60–80 MB, on Wi-Fi).
  • Full help: the ? button in the app (English / Hebrew).

Desktop replay

Open the desktop replay in any browser and drop a flight-log CSV: the same phone-shaped screen, colours and panel; plays the recorded flight at 30/120/600×. Works offline (except optional imagery). Also downloadable as one file: docs/replay/index.html.

Three ways to replay a flight: in the app (Settings → Flight logs → tap a log → Replay); online here (replay/, drop the CSV you shared from the app); or offline — save the replay file once and double-click it. Only the optional imagery needs a network.

What the app does

  • Offline world map (Natural Earth) with countries, cities and airports.
  • Distance flown and remaining, speed, altitude, track, time to go, arrival in the destination's clock, time at origin and UTC.
  • With GPS the aircraft is where it was measured; without GPS it continues along the route from the last known point, and the track is drawn dashed.
  • Every value is marked: measured ● / fused ◐ / predicted ○ / stale ◌. Aircraft ring: green = good GPS, orange = weak, red = estimated.
  • CSV flight log on the phone (optional). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
